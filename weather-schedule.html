Weather Work Window

Choose your trade and city. In under a minute you see which days this week are good for outdoor work, and get ready-to-send messages for customers whose appointments may need to move. No sign-up needed.

This is a prototype built for a challenge. The day ratings use simple rules of thumb for each trade, which are assumptions and not safety advice. Always check the product data sheet, the current forecast and your safety rules before you decide. The AI can make mistakes, so please read the messages before you send them.
